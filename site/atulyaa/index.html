How a bag is made

From the wheat truck to your shop

Every step is recorded in our mill system, so we can trace any bag back to its batch.

  1. Weighed in

    Each wheat truck is weighed at our gate on arrival.

  2. Lab tested

    Moisture and foreign matter are checked before unloading.

  3. Cleaned and milled

    Wheat is cleaned, then ground into atta, maida, sooji and chokar.

  4. Packed and batched

    Bags are packed and given a batch number the same day.

  5. Billed and delivered

    Loaded on our truck with a GST bill and delivered to you.
