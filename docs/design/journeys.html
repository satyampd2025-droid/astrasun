Atulyaa Mill OS · MVP journeys

How each person uses the app, step by step

Eight journeys follow one truck of wheat through to one customer's payment. Each screen shows how every input is captured. Most fields are filled by the system or picked with a tap; typing is kept for the few things only a person knows.

Gate → Weighbridge → QC → Raw wheat → Production → Bulk atta → Packing → Finished bags → Sales order → Approval → Loading → Invoice + e-way bill → Dispatch → Payment
Input rules1 Gate & weighbridge2 QC3 Production4 Packing5 Sales order6 Approval7 Loading & dispatch8 Payment

Don't ask for what the system knows

Vehicle number brings up the supplier and purchase order. Customer brings up price list, credit and payment terms. Lot IDs, batch IDs, totals and GST are generated.

Pick before type

Anything with a short list of options (shift, SKU, QC decision, payment mode, reasons) is a chip or tile tap.

Numbers on a big numpad

Weights, counts and rates open a numpad with the unit shown. Values outside the normal range turn amber and ask for a second tap to confirm.

Proof by photo, reasons by voice

Weighbridge slips, supplier bills and cheques get a photo. Reasons and remarks can be spoken in Hindi and saved as text and audio.

One job per screen

Each role opens on its own to-do list. One main button at the bottom, within thumb reach. Hindi and English on every screen.

Phone for the floor, web for the office

These journeys are the phone app. Accounts, masters, reports and settings run on the web screens that come with ERPNext.

Decisions and assumptions

  • Decided: ERPNext is the accounting system (no separate Tally books).
  • Decided: invoice and e-way bill are made before the truck leaves (journey 7).
  • Decided: Hindi, voice input and WhatsApp alerts and receipts are in the MVP.
  • Still open: offline use. The requirements document puts it after the MVP. If it moves into the MVP, gate, loading and packing screens would save on the phone and send when the network returns.
  • Weighbridge weights are typed with a slip photo for the MVP; a direct connection comes later.
  • Payment collection (journey 8) is added to the MVP scope; it's needed for dues to update.
  • Wheat purchase orders, masters, expenses, reports and settings are done on the web and aren't shown here.
  • All names, quantities and rates on these screens are examples.
