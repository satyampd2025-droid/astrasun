# Astrasun: Aata Mill App (PRD)

**Mockups:** https://www.figma.com/design/huj5alfaNPY5mUAxb2z54p

## What it is
- One app to run the whole mill: wheat in → flour out → sold → paid.
- Everyone uses it: owner, manager, sales, loading, drivers, accounts.
- Everyone stays updated, and the owner gets smart insights.

## Problems today
- Registers, WhatsApp, and phone calls: no single record.
- The owner hears about problems late.
- Weight, stock, and bag losses go unnoticed.
- Decisions are made on gut feel, not data.

## Who uses it
| Person | What they do in the app |
|---|---|
| Owner | See dashboard, approve orders, get alerts and insights |
| Manager | Plan production, track trucks, stock, and tasks |
| Purchase | Wheat orders, supplier rates |
| Gate / Weighbridge | Truck entry, weight in/out |
| Lab | Wheat and flour quality check |
| Mill operator | Log production and downtime |
| Packing / Store | Bags packed, stock |
| Sales rep | Take orders, check stock and customer dues |
| Dispatch | Assign orders to trucks |
| Loading staff | See "load X bags on truck Y", tap done |
| Driver | Delivery photo, cash collected |
| Accounts | Bills, payments, dues |

## Main flow
1. Wheat truck arrives → gate entry → weigh → quality check → unload.
2. Mill runs → aata / maida / sooji / chokar recorded.
3. Bags packed → stock updates automatically.
4. Sales rep books order → owner approves if over credit.
5. Loading task → loaded → bill + e-way bill → truck leaves.
6. Delivered → payment collected → dues updated.

## Must-have features (v1)
- Role-based home screen: "what I need to do now".
- Live stock (wheat, flour, bags).
- Sales orders with credit check.
- Loading and dispatch tasks.
- GST bills and payment tracking.
- Notifications on app and WhatsApp.
- Hindi and English, voice input, big buttons.
- Works offline.

## Alerts (who gets what)
- Truck at gate → purchase, lab.
- Weight mismatch → owner, manager.
- Order needs approval → owner (approve in 1 tap).
- Loading task → loader.
- Truck dispatched → sales rep and customer.
- Payment overdue → sales rep and accounts.
- Low stock → store, purchase.
- Machine down → maintenance, manager.
- Every evening: day summary on WhatsApp to the owner.

## Smart insights
- Today's sales, production, collections, stock.
- Extraction % (flour from wheat) per shift.
- Profit per product.
- Cost per ton (wheat, power, labour, packing).
- Who owes money and for how long.
- Unusual activity flagged (weight gaps, low yield, power spikes).
- Later: demand forecast, when to buy wheat, price suggestions.
- Later: ask the app in Hindi, e.g. "Kaun sabse zyada baaki hai?" ("Who owes the most?")

## Later (v2+)
- Direct weighbridge connection.
- Tally sync, e-invoice.
- Barcode/QR on bags.
- Maintenance and attendance.
- Customer WhatsApp ordering.

## Success looks like
- 90% of staff use it every week.
- 70% fewer "what's the status?" calls to the owner.
- Faster truck turnaround and dispatch.
- Fewer old dues (60+ days).

## Plan
- 3 weeks: understand current process, collect data.
- 10 weeks: build v1.
- 4 weeks: pilot alongside registers, then switch over.
